# Client Research Brief

## Business

Express Pizza & Scoops is a Newington neighborhood restaurant at 82 Pane Road. Public sources associate it with pizza, wings, grinders, sandwiches, dinners, desserts, beverages, and an ice-cream/scoops offering, though the exact current scope requires confirmation.

## Current Digital Position

Express is already discoverable and orderable across several major platforms. A Slice-powered page provides a direct menu, pickup/delivery paths, phone access, directions, and a large catalog. This is a meaningful operational strength.

## Current Digital Weakness

The customer-facing brand is dependent on outside platforms and is split between Express Pizza & Scoops and the legacy Best Pizza name. Hours, phone numbers, categories, prices, and ordering labels differ across sources. The business lacks a clear owned home that explains who it is before sending the customer to checkout.

## Customer

Likely visitors include hungry local residents on mobile, returning customers looking for the menu, families ordering bundles, pickup customers, delivery customers comparing options, and customers looking for a nearby dessert or ice-cream stop.

## Primary Customer Intent

The primary intent is to answer five questions quickly: Is Express open? What do they serve? How much does it cost? Can I order for pickup or delivery? Where is it and how do I call?

## Biggest Opportunity

Create a client-owned digital front door that resolves the name transition, makes the pizza-plus-scoops identity memorable, and routes visitors to the verified ordering destinations with minimal friction.

## Secondary Opportunities

Secondary opportunities include a verified menu summary, catering/large-order visibility if confirmed, local SEO cleanup, a photography refresh, review/profile links, and a single authoritative NAP record.

## Business Risk

If nothing changes, marketplace pages will continue to define the brand, the legacy Best Pizza identity may remain in search, and customers may encounter conflicting hours, phone numbers, or prices. The risk is lost confidence and weaker direct brand recall rather than an absence of demand.

## Forge Opportunity

Forge-CT can provide the owned marketing layer: a focused responsive site, clear brand system, structured local content, mobile CTAs, verified information, and careful links into the existing ordering stack. Forge should present this as an enhancement to what already works.
