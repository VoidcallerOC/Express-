# Content Architecture

## Recommended pages

### Home

Purpose: establish the current Express Pizza & Scoops identity and route visitors to order, menu, call, and directions. Content should include a concise hero, verified service modes, selected menu highlights, hours, address, phone, and local CTA.

### Menu

Purpose: orient the customer without duplicating the full third-party checkout. Use only categories observed in current sources, with a clear note that current prices and modifiers are confirmed in the ordering system.

Verified category set: Family Deals; Pickup Specials; Pizza; Specialty Pizza; Appetizers; Salads; Breads; Buffalo Wings; Chicken Tenders; Calzones; Stromboli; Deluxe Sandwiches; Triple Decker Club; Wraps; Pita Pockets; Grinders; Parmigiana Grinders; Hot Dogs; Paninis; Quesadillas; Chef’s Specials; Dinners; Specialty Dinners; Fried Dinners; Kids Menu; Desserts; Beverages; Lunch Specials; Salads Catering; Dinners Catering. Uber Eats also exposes Soup, Burgers, Express Specialties, and Icecream; these require client confirmation before inclusion in the canonical site menu. [1] [2]

### Order Online

Purpose: explain that Express uses existing external ordering infrastructure and provide the confirmed preferred links for pickup, delivery, and any marketplace alternatives. Do not create a local checkout.

### About

Purpose: introduce the business, neighborhood context, and pizza-plus-scoops identity. Use only client-approved history and claims. This page can be omitted if the owner prefers a compact one-page site, but its content should still exist somewhere.

### Contact / Location

Purpose: make the address, phone, hours, directions, parking/access notes, pickup/delivery information, and map easy to use on mobile.

### Catering (conditional)

Create only if the owner confirms active catering, menus, lead time, and an inquiry process. Current public sources show catering categories, but public menu availability alone is not proof of an actively promoted catering program.

## Navigation

Use a short header navigation with Order Online as the visually dominant action. Footer should repeat address, phone, hours, ordering link, directions, social links, and a concise menu of pages.

## Content rules

No invented testimonials, prices, categories, hours, delivery radius, award claims, ingredient claims, or ownership history. Mark any client-supplied menu as the dated source of truth.
