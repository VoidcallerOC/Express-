# SEO Strategy

## Primary local search intent

The primary intent is a nearby customer searching for pizza in Newington, Connecticut and needing a fast answer about menu, hours, ordering, pickup, delivery, phone, or directions. The site should target this intent naturally through page titles, visible copy, and structured local information.

## Secondary intents

Use only verified offerings for supporting intent: pizza pickup/delivery in Newington, wings, grinders, family pizza deals, desserts, and ice cream/scoops. Do not optimize for catering, gluten-free, or other categories until the client confirms active availability.

## Metadata strategy

Use a canonical page title such as “Express Pizza & Scoops | Pizza, Pickup & Delivery in Newington, CT” after the name is confirmed. Descriptions should mention the address area, menu, ordering, phone, and current service modes without keyword stuffing. Each page should have one descriptive H1 and unique metadata.

## Structured data

Implement `Restaurant` or `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD only after confirmation of name, address, phone, URL, hours, price range, menu URL, and social profiles. Add `sameAs` only for verified official profiles. Do not encode conflicting hours or marketplace review ratings as if they were owned-site facts.

## Location strategy

The Contact/Location page should show 82 Pane Rd, Newington, CT 06111, the confirmed phone, weekly hours, directions link, and pickup/delivery explanation. Keep NAP identical across the site and request updates to major third-party listings after the client approves the canonical record.

## Internal linking

Home should link to Menu, Order Online, and Contact. Menu should link to Order Online. Every page should expose phone and directions. If Catering is confirmed, link it from Home, Menu, and footer.

## Sitemap and technical requirements

Provide a small XML sitemap covering only indexable pages. Set canonical URLs, robots rules, descriptive image filenames, alt text, Open Graph metadata, and a valid favicon. Do not index thin redirect pages or duplicate third-party menus.

## Image SEO

Use current client-approved imagery, descriptive alt text, responsive sizes, and modern formats. Do not use alt text as a keyword list. Attribute or omit public legacy images until rights are confirmed.

## Local content opportunities

Useful content includes a clear “how to order” explanation, a verified pickup/delivery service note, family-deal orientation, and a short local contact/location section. Avoid fabricated neighborhood claims, “best” claims, and thin blog posts written only for keywords.
