# Final Audit Report

## Executive Summary

Express Pizza & Scoops has a real and active digital presence, but not a clearly verified owned website. Customers can find and order from the business through Slice, Uber Eats, Grubhub, DoorDash, Postmates, legacy Best Pizza properties, and social/listing pages. The strongest opportunity is therefore not “create an online presence.” It is to create a branded, client-owned front door that consolidates the current identity, presents a verified menu orientation, and routes customers to the ordering systems that already work. The key risks are brand-transition confusion, conflicting hours and phone information, platform-specific pricing, and uncertain ownership or currentness of public visual assets.

## Biggest Opportunity

Build an owned digital home that makes Express Pizza & Scoops the organizing layer before the customer reaches Slice or another ordering platform.

## Biggest Risk

Customers may encounter an outdated Best Pizza identity or conflicting phone and hours information, causing hesitation, misdirected calls, or missed orders.

## Forge Value Proposition

Forge-CT can improve the first impression and customer path without replacing the business’s existing checkout infrastructure. The deliverable is a focused, fast, local, mobile-first brand home with verified information and clear external ordering handoffs.

## Recommended Site

A compact responsive site with Home, Menu, Order Online, About, and Contact/Location. Add Catering only after confirmation. The homepage should lead with approved Express branding, Pizza + Scoops positioning if confirmed, order/menu/call/directions CTAs, verified hours, and location. The menu page should orient customers and link to the full external menu rather than duplicate a POS.

## Required Assets

Approved logo files, canonical business name, brand colors, typography preferences, current food and storefront photography, social URLs, current menu and pricing source, preferred ordering links, verified hours, phone, and permission for any legacy imagery.

## Client Questions

What is the canonical public business name? Is Best Pizza retired, retained, or a transition alias? Which phone number is current? What are the exact weekly and holiday hours? Is Scoops/ice cream an active core offering? Which menu and prices are authoritative? Which ordering links should be primary? Is delivery offered directly or only through marketplaces? Is catering active? Which photos, logo, social accounts, and domain are approved?

## Verified Facts

The address is repeatedly listed as 82 Pane Road, Newington, CT 06111. The phone (860) 665-8128 is repeated by current Slice, Grubhub, Uber Eats, and the Slice-powered order page. Current public ordering sources show a broad pizza, wings, grinders, sandwich, dinner, dessert, beverage, and catering menu. The Slice-powered page reports pickup and delivery ordering, with external platform dependence.

## Unverified Facts

The definitive legal/display name, current hours, current phone if legacy listings are considered, current prices, delivery radius, active scoops inventory, catering status, official social accounts, asset rights, and exact relationship between Best Pizza and Express are not fully verified.

## Phase 2 Build Recommendation

Start from the empty repository with a fresh, minimal architecture. Do not migrate legacy code because none exists. First obtain client confirmation for identity, NAP, menu, ordering links, and assets. Then implement the owned front door, preserve external checkout, run mobile/accessibility/performance checks, and update only the approved public information.
