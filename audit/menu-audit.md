# Menu Audit

## Source hierarchy

The Slice-powered ordering page and Slice menu are the most coherent current menu sources because they expose a full category structure and item descriptions. Uber Eats and Grubhub are useful cross-checks but may apply platform-specific prices, promotions, availability, and category labels. [1] [2] [3]

## Verified category structure

The current Slice sources expose: Family Deals; Pickup Specials; Pizza; Specialty Pizza; Appetizers; Salads; Breads; Buffalo Wings; Chicken Tenders; Calzones; Stromboli; Deluxe Sandwiches; Triple Decker Club; Wraps; Pita Pockets; Grinders; Parmigiana Grinders; Hot Dogs; Paninis; Quesadillas; Chef’s Specials; Dinners; Specialty Dinners; Fried Dinners; Kids Menu; Desserts; Beverages; Lunch Specials; Salads Catering; Dinners Catering.

Uber Eats also exposes Appetizers, Soup, Salads, Club Sandwich, Burgers, Wraps, Quesadillas, Pizza, White Pizza Specialties, Gluten Free, Calzone and Stromboli, Fried Dinners, Dinners, Parmesans, Express Specialties, Buffalo Wings, Hot Grinders, Cold Grinders, Lunch Special, Kids Menu, Desserts, Beverages, and Icecream. The overlap is high, but the canonical category naming is not identical. [3]

## Pricing conflicts

The same or similar items vary by platform. Cheese pizza is $12.99 on Slice and $12.50 on Uber. Delivery marketplace prices may include channel pricing, promotions, or different item configurations. The Phase 2 site should either omit prices from marketing summaries or use a client-approved dated menu source with a clear change policy.
