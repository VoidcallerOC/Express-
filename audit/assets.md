## Asset recommendations

Before Phase 2, request an approved logo in SVG/PNG, brand colors, typography preferences, current food/storefront photos, social handles, and written permission for any legacy imagery. Confirm whether “Scoops” has current product photography and whether ice cream is a priority product line.

## Prohibited assumptions

Do not fabricate a logo, redraw legacy branding, scrape and republish customer photos, or treat a public URL as evidence of reuse rights. Do not use “Best Pizza” assets as the Express brand without explicit approval.
