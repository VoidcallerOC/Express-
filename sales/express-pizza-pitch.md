# Forge-CT Sales Angle: Express Pizza & Scoops

## Opening pitch

“You already have customers finding and ordering from you online. I built out what it could look like if Express had its own digital home.”

## Problem

Today, the customer can find Express across ordering platforms, but the first impression is fragmented. The public footprint still mixes Express Pizza & Scoops with the older Best Pizza name, while hours, phone numbers, menus, and prices vary by platform. That makes it harder for a customer to know which information to trust.

## Opportunity

The opportunity is not to throw away the platforms that already deliver orders. It is to add the branded front door that comes before them:

**Current:** Search → third-party platform → menu → order.

**Forge:** Search → Express-owned website → menu → order → call → directions.

## Value

A branded site can help Express control the first impression, make the Pizza + Scoops identity memorable, reduce confusion around hours and location, show the right menu categories, and send customers to the ordering channel that already works.

## What Forge would build

Forge would build a mobile-first owned home with approved branding, a clear menu orientation, Order Online routing, call and directions actions, verified local SEO data, a location page, and a simple content system that the business can keep accurate. Forge would not pretend to replace the restaurant’s POS or create a fake checkout.

## What the client would provide

The client would confirm the current business name, phone, hours, menu, prices, pickup/delivery rules, ordering links, preferred social profiles, approved logo, current photos, and any legacy Best Pizza transition language.

## Suggested next conversation

Ask the owner to walk through a recent customer journey on a phone. Then confirm which information is authoritative today and which public listings need cleanup. Review a small Phase 2 scope focused on the owned front door rather than a replacement ordering system.

## Objection handling

**“We already have Slice/Uber Eats/Grubhub.”** Exactly. Forge can keep those systems and make them easier to reach from a branded site.

**“We do not need another place to manage.”** The site can stay intentionally small. Its job is to keep the canonical information and route orders, calls, and directions.

**“Our customers know us already.”** The site helps new customers and returning customers get the same correct answer quickly, especially from mobile search.

**“Will this replace our ordering system?”** No. The recommended approach leaves the existing order infrastructure in place.

**“We had a Best Pizza website.”** That is useful transition context. The next step is to confirm what should be preserved and what should now represent Express Pizza & Scoops.

## Suggested CTA

“Let’s spend 20 minutes confirming the current menu, brand name, hours, and ordering links. Then I can show you the exact owned-home structure without changing the systems that already take orders.”
